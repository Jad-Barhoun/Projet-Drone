# List of electric components

## Drone

* **1 Microcontroller:** [STM32F411RET6](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32f411re.html) ([Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f411ce.pdf))

* **1 IMU:** [LSM6DSO](https://www.st.com/en/mems-and-sensors/lsm6dso.html) ([Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/lsm6dso.pdf))

* **3 Proximity sensors:** [VL53L1X](https://www.st.com/en/imaging-and-photonics-solutions/vl53l1x) ([Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/vl53l1x.pdf))

* **1 Radio module:** nRF24L01+ module ([Nordic Semiconductor](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/nRF24-series))

* **4 MOSFETs (motor):** [MOSFET-N SI2302](https://www.vishay.com/en/product/71831/) ([Datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/71831/si2302ad.pdf))

* **4 Flyback diodes (motor):** [SS14](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/5fe7630942b4fda616dbbd5c10da9292.pdf)

* **4 10 kΩ SMD resistors (MOSFET gate-source, motor)**

* **4 (2 CW + 2 CCW) brushed motors:** 7x16 mm, 0.8 mm shaft, 19000 KV [BetaFPV](https://betafpv.com/products/7x16mm-19000kv-brushed-motors-2cw-2ccw)

* **1 Push button**

* **2 LED:** status LED (2 colors: Green and Red)

* **1 Switch:** power switch

* **1 Buzzer** 

* **1 ST-LINK/V2:** for programming and debugging the STM32 through SWD

## RC transmitter

* **1 Microcontroller:** [STM32F401RET6](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32f401re.html) ([Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f401re.pdf))

* **1 Radio module:** nRF24L01+ module ([Nordic Semiconductor](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/nRF24-series))

* **2 Joysticks:** KY-023 dual-axis analog joystick module

* **6 Push buttons**

* **2 LEDs** (2 colors: Green and Red)

* **1 Switch**

* **1 Buzzer**

## Miscellaneous

* **1 ST-LINK/V2:** for programming and debugging the STM32 through SWD
